# Moncls Paid-Search Strategy

## Business
Monumental Chauffeured Limousine Service (monumentallimo.com → moncls.com):
D.C.-based premium chauffeured / VIP ground transportation. Airport transfers,
executive/corporate car service, embassy & diplomatic delegation transport, and
multi-day event ground-logistics programs (World Bank/IMF, AUSA, board
meetings). Core geo: D.C. Metro, Baltimore, Northern Virginia — expanding
nationally via affiliates. Serves both **daily point-to-point** and **multi-day
event bookings**.

## What the historical data showed
Sources: a 2012 Keyword Planner export, a 2023 Zoho email-campaign audit log,
and 2025 strategy docs. **No Google Ads performance data exists** (no spend,
CTR, conversions, CPA). Old account: `6252549213`.

- The only Ads artifact: one campaign, one ad group, ~68 **broad-match**
  keywords — premium DC terms mixed with a large block of UK "car hire,"
  "cheap limo," "discount car hire" terms chasing 100k+ volume.
- 2025 doc says paid ads were "experimental / not actively running" and
  "have not proven effective… leads skew price-sensitive or one-time."
- Real wins were **referrals** (BAE Systems, 14+ yrs) and an **Apollo/email
  campaign** around World Bank 2023 (landed Earthbound, Cotur).

## Diagnosis
Paid search didn't fail as a channel — the 2012 account was structured to
attract the wrong customer (broad match + bargain/off-geo terms → price
shoppers). The new campaign must buy the **high-intent corporate / premium**
customer instead.

## New-campaign direction (draft — validate against live data once available)
- **Two intent clusters**, matching the business:
  1. *Multi-day / event*: "corporate event transportation dc", "conference
     shuttle service", "delegation ground transportation", "roadshow car
     service".
  2. *Daily / point-to-point*: "executive car service dc", "airport car
     service dulles/dca/iad", "chauffeur service washington dc".
- **Match types**: phrase/exact only at launch. No broad without a strong
  negative list.
- **Negatives from day one**: cheap, discount, rental, hire, uber, lyft, jobs,
  salary, + off-geo (UK/other-city terms).
- **Geo**: tiered — D.C./Baltimore/NoVA at full bid; national only on
  high-value corporate/event terms.
- **Conversions**: must be tracked before scaling (call + form + booking).
  This is the #1 missing prerequisite.

## Open prerequisites
1. Basic Access on developer token (production data).
2. Conversion tracking live on moncls.com.
3. Landing pages per intent cluster (event vs. daily).
